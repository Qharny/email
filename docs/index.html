Email Sender

A minimal browser extension for sending one email to many people from your own Gmail, Outlook or SMTP accounts. Each recipient gets their own copy, personalised with their name.

Download extension Install guide Source code

What it does

  • Sends from one or more of your own accounts: Gmail through Google sign-in, others through a small companion app on your computer.
  • Sends each recipient an individual email, filling in {name} and {email}.
  • Works in Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave.
  • Has no server: messages go straight from your browser to Gmail or your mail provider.
